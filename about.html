History, Constitution, and the Vision for 2050.

Our History

The Telangana Athletics Association was formed on 1-6-2014 with 10 District associations as members after the bifurcation of the state from Andhra Pradesh. The newly formed association started to function to its full capacity from 2-6-2014.

The first elected body was formed on 1-6-2015 with Mr. Sabyasachi Ghosh IAS as President, Mr. K. Ranga Rao as Secretary, and Mr. B.E. Stanley Jones as Treasurer. Later, the Government of Telangana increased the number of districts to 33. The Telangana Athletics Association formed a committee to establish new associations in these districts; the committee has successfully formed all the 33 district associations.

"The Athletics Association of former unified A.P. and present Telangana acknowledge the service rendered by Prof. K. Ranga Rao for his selfless service to the field of Athletics."

The present Executive Committee was formed on 28-12-2018 with Mr. B.E. Stanley Jones as President, Mr. K. Sarangapani as Secretary, and Dr. Rajesh Kumar as Treasurer.

Our Mission & Vision

The Telangana Athletics Association is deeply committed to the development and promotion of athletics across the state. Our primary objective is to identify promising young talent at the grassroots level, providing them with quality training, mentorship, and support to compete regionally, nationally, and internationally.

Looking ahead, our vision is to transform Telangana into a global sports hub by 2050. We aim to nurture talent through the establishment of specialized sports academies, focusing on long-term athlete development.
