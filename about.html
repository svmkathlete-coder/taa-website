About Us

The Telangana Athletics Association is deeply committed to the development and promotion of athletics across the state. Our primary objective is to identify promising young talent at the grassroots level, providing them with quality training, mentorship, and support to compete regionally, nationally, and internationally.

Looking ahead, our vision is to transform Telangana into a global sports hub by 2050. We aim to nurture talent through the establishment of specialized sports academies, focusing on long-term athlete development.

Since our formation, the association has worked tirelessly to establish governance structures across the state. We have successfully expanded to form 33 district associations, ensuring representation and opportunity for athletes throughout Telangana.
